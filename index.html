What Overlay does

Two hotkeys, one small window, and no subscription.

Dictation hotkey

Press your shortcut, speak, and your words are typed into whatever app you're using. Speech-to-text runs on your PC with local Whisper, so it stays private and works offline.

Assistant hotkey

Ask a question out loud and get just the answer in a small overlay on top of your work. It can search the web and shows its sources.

Safe PC actions

Say things like “open Notepad” or “turn the volume down”. Only actions on an allowlist can run, so the assistant can't do anything you haven't allowed.

Hinglish support

Speak the way you actually talk, mixing Hindi and English. Overlay can write it out in Roman script (Hinglish).

Pick your answer source

Use a local Ollama model, an OpenClaw gateway, Gemini with Google Search, or Tavily. Switch whenever you like.

Free and local-first

Every option is free to use. Wherever possible things run on your own machine, in a clean maroon-themed interface.

How it works

Set it up once, then it's always one keypress away.

  1. 1

    Press your hotkey

    Pick a shortcut like Ctrl+Shift+Space. Hotkeys are tap-to-toggle: tap once to start, tap again to stop.

  2. 2

    Speak naturally

    English, Hindi, or a mix of both. Whisper turns your voice into text right on your PC.

  3. 3

    Get text or an answer

    Dictation types the text into your current app. The assistant shows the answer, with sources when it searched the web.

Choose how it answers

The assistant can get its answers from any of these. All of them have a free way to use them.

Private

Local Ollama model

Runs a model on your own PC. Free, and your questions don't leave your machine.

Self-hosted

OpenClaw gateway

Point Overlay at your own OpenClaw gateway and use whatever it's set up to answer with.

Web search

Gemini + Google Search

Use Gemini with Google Search grounding for up-to-date answers with sources.

Web search

Tavily

A search API built for AI answers, for fresh results with links.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Overlay is open source under the MIT license and costs nothing. Every answer source it supports has a free option. Some online services (like Gemini or Tavily) need your own free API key and have their own usage limits.

Does it work offline?

Dictation does: speech-to-text runs on your PC with local Whisper once the model is downloaded. The assistant also works offline with a local Ollama model. Web search answers and the online sources (Gemini, Tavily) need an internet connection.

Is it Windows only?

Yes, for now. Overlay is built and tested on Windows. Hotkeys use tap-to-toggle mode (tap to start, tap again to stop).

Is it a fork?

Yes. Overlay is built on OpenWhispr (MIT, Gizmo Labs Inc.). The original license and credit are kept. Overlay adds the assistant overlay with web search and sources, the selectable answer sources, safe PC actions, Hinglish support, and a new maroon look.

Where does my voice go?

For dictation, your audio is turned into text on your own PC. If you pick an online answer source for the assistant, your question is sent to that service.
